**Thresholds** gathers a year of new paintings by North Country artist **June Hart**, each circling the same idea: the charged moment just before you cross from one place — or state — into another.

Doorways, porch steps, the edge of a tree line at dusk. Hart renders these in-between spaces in thick, warm light, inviting you to stand a while on the threshold before deciding whether to step through.

The show runs through mid-August. Stop by during open hours, or catch the artist at the [opening reception](/events/).
