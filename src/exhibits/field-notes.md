This fall, **Field Notes** brings together a group of North Country artists who all work from direct observation — sketchbooks in pockets, paint boxes in the truck.

Landscape, weather, the St. Lawrence, the changing season: the show is a collective record of paying attention to this specific place. Opening details to come — [follow along](/events/) for the reception announcement.
