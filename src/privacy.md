Phi Gallery's website uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand, in aggregate, which pages and events people are interested in. That helps us program better shows and know that our listings are reaching the North Country.

- We collect **anonymized, aggregate** usage data (pages viewed, rough region, whether you tapped "directions" or a ticket link). IP addresses are anonymized.
- We **don't** sell your data, run ad trackers, or try to identify you personally.
- We don't require a cookie-consent banner for this basic analytics, but you're always free to block analytics with your browser or an extension — the site works exactly the same either way.

If you email us or fill out a form, we only use what you send to reply to you.
