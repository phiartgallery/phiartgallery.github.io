Our rotating collective of local artisans takes over the gallery and spills out onto the Square. Expect ceramics, hand-bound books, jewelry, prints, plants, baked goods, and finds from **Faeried Treasure Trove** and friends.

Free to browse, all day. Come support North Country makers and grab something one-of-a-kind.
