Settle in for an evening of restorative sound. Our resident practitioners from **WildRoots Wellness and Revival** guide a full sound bath with crystal singing bowls, gongs, and chimes — a chance to slow down and reset in the middle of the gallery.

Bring a yoga mat, a blanket, and anything that helps you get comfortable on the floor. Doors at 6:30, session begins promptly at 7. Space is limited, so arrive early.

Free to attend — donations to the artists are always welcome.
