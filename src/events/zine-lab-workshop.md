Fold, cut, draw, photocopy, repeat. In this hands-on literary-arts workshop, **Dana Gillan** walks you through making a one-page mini-zine from start to finish — no experience needed.

All materials and the copier are provided. Make as many as you like, leave one in the community trade box, and take home a stack of everyone else's.

Great for kids and adults alike. Drop in any time between 1 and 4pm.
