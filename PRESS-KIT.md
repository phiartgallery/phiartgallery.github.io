## Full boilerplate (~150 words)

Phi Gallery is a community art space opened in 2023 by Stow Dunham on Public Square in downtown Watertown, NY. Built by artists, for artists, the gallery is home to visual arts and exhibits, musical artists and events, literary arts professionals and workshops, sound healing, and more — a DIY hub where the North Country's creative community gathers, shows work, and plays shows.

Phi Gallery also houses a collective of local makers and organizations, including WildRoots Wellness and Revival, Holdown Upstate, Local Legendz by Tunes 92.5, Faeried Treasure Trove, and a handful of incredible local artisanal vendors.

The gallery is located at 88 Public Square, Watertown, NY 13601, and is coordinated by Stow Dunham, Dana Gillan, and The Myricks (Mykel "Quince" Myrick & Destiny Myrick).
